Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Daniel Bydžovský, a private individual in the Czech Republic. Contact: cheprsn@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, display name and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email and name.
  • Fishing sessions: start and end time, active time, GPS route and start location, distance, step count, detected casts and fights, and catches you record.
  • Spots: places where you fished, derived from the start of your sessions. Spots are private and never shown to other users.
  • Rod sensor: the identifier and name of the sensor you pair.
  • Social: friend requests and friendships.
  • Photos: catch photos you choose to upload.
  • Technical data: IP address and request logs kept by our hosting provider for security.

Location is used only while a session is recording. On Android a notification is shown for as long as recording runs; on iOS the system shows the location indicator.

Why we use it

  • To provide the app: record, store and show your sessions (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Who can see your data

Each session has a visibility you choose: private (only you), friends (people you have mutually accepted as friends) or public (any CastTrail user). Your display name is visible to other signed-in users. Spots are always private.

We use these service providers:

  • Supabase – database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Google – only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation – map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers, which receive your IP address and the map area being shown.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Sessions recorded offline stay on your phone until they are uploaded. When you delete your account, your profile and sessions are hidden from others immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days, including photos. Signing in again within those 30 days cancels the deletion. See Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, a copy in a portable format, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or object to processing. Write to cheprsn@gmail.com; we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz.

Children

CastTrail is not intended for children under 15.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
