Your fishing sessions, logged by the rod.

A small sensor on your rod counts every cast and spots every fight. CastTrail turns it into a session with your route, active time and catches, while your phone stays in your pocket.

Join the beta How it works

Fish. CastTrail does the logging.

No buttons to press at the water. Everything is recorded automatically and you can fill in the details later.

Casts and fights

The rod sensor counts casts and detects fish fights. A fight is logged as a catch automatically.

Phone in your pocket

Recording runs in the background with the screen off. Turn the rod on and the session starts.

Route and active time

Your GPS route and steps are tracked. Pauses are detected, so only real fishing time counts.

Catch log

Add species, length, weight, a photo and the bait whenever it suits you. Every field is optional.

Private spots

Your spots are built from where you fish and stay yours. Exact places are never shown to anyone.

Share with friends

Post sessions to your friends or to everyone. You choose for each session, and private is always an option.

How it works

  1. Pair once

    Attach the sensor to your rod and pair it in three steps. After that it connects on its own.

  2. Turn on the rod and fish

    The session starts automatically. It works offline and syncs when you are back online.

  3. Review and share

    Look back at the route, casts and catches, fill in the details and share it if you like.
