Common questions

My session is not on the server yet. Sessions are saved on your phone first and upload automatically when you are signed in and online.

Why can't I sign out? Sign-out waits until all sessions have uploaded, so nothing recorded offline is lost. Go online and try again.

The rod sensor does not connect. Turn on Bluetooth on your phone, turn the sensor on and keep it close to the phone.

How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.
