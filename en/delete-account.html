Delete your CastTrail account

CastTrail is developed by Daniel Bydžovský.

In the app

  1. Open the You tab.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email cheprsn@gmail.com from the address you use for CastTrail with the subject "Delete my CastTrail account". We confirm and process the request within 7 days.

What happens

  • You are signed out on all devices, and your profile and sessions are hidden from other users right away.
  • After 30 days, your account and all its data are permanently deleted: profile, sessions, routes, events, catches, photos, spots, paired sensors and friendships.
  • Signing in again within those 30 days cancels the deletion.
  • Sessions on your phone that have not uploaded yet are deleted from the phone immediately.

We do not keep any of your data after the deletion is complete.
